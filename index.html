About Me

Welcome! I am a computational biology researcher focused on leveraging computational methods and mathematical modeling to solve complex biological problems.

My work involves developing and applying algorithms, statistical methods, and machine learning techniques to analyze biological data and gain insights into biological systems.

Research Interests

Genomics & Bioinformatics

Analysis of genomic data, sequence alignment, and variant calling using computational approaches.

Systems Biology

Mathematical modeling of biological systems and networks to understand cellular processes.

Machine Learning in Biology

Application of machine learning and deep learning methods to biological data analysis.

Structural Biology

Computational protein structure prediction and analysis of protein-protein interactions.
